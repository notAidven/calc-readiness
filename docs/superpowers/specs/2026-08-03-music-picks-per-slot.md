## The attribution text, literally

**CC0 owes nothing**; those rows need only the credit `CREDITS.md:8-11` gives CC0 authors as house
policy, with the artist named above. **The three CC-BY rows owe exactly this.** The wording and
line breaks are Kevin MacLeod's own, read off `incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html` on
2026-08-03, where he requires the credit be placed so *"a person who wants to know where the music
came from should have no difficulty in finding it"*. The fifth line is CC-BY 4.0's
indicate-changes term, which all three trigger because each needs a loop edit and a re-encode.

**Shipping any CC-BY row costs a product change that does not exist.** `CREDITS.md` is a repository
file, not a learner-visible surface, so a findable credit means adding a credits screen. Answering
"CC0 only" is a real option and costs exactly these three rows: **era 1** falls back to CC0 options
every pass that examined them flagged or rejected, **era 8** to
[Empty Stretch](https://opengameart.org/content/empty-stretch) (Zane Little Music, CC0 1.0), which
the pass that found it called adjacent to the era rather than in it, and the **Succession** goes
empty again.

## Notes, where a row needs one

- **`era-neolithic` is genuinely empty and should be generated.** The two strongest of the five
  candidates ever proposed are disqualified by properties printed on their own source rows:
  `Accralate`'s catalogue description reads *"Feel change in the middle; Tempo change near the end"*
  and names agogo and cowbell, both metal, which era two bans outright; `Krakatoa` is 72 seconds
  against a 90-second floor. The fourth pass then enumerated all 28 CC0 tuned-wood assets on
  OpenGameArt and found nothing era two's.
- **`era-prehistoric`**: second choice is [Lotus](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/Lotus.mp3) (CC-BY 4.0), whose author disclaims five cultures by name — era one's hardest ban — but whose published 98 bpm misses its 60–72 band badly.
- **`era-bronze_age`**: the artist is Umplix, and his other track here was excluded by an earlier pass for being *"made almost entirely out of Apple loops"*; on this one he writes *"I did not use any Apple Loops on this one"*, and his word is the only evidence. Second choice [That Zen Moment](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/That%20Zen%20Moment.mp3) (CC-BY 4.0), a struck metal bowl drum.
- **`era-classical`**: second choice [Meadow Thoughts](https://opengameart.org/content/meadow-thoughts) (mishonis, CC0 1.0) is solo harp and the only candidate inside era four's own 120–200 second window.
- **`era-medieval`**: it is a town theme like both tracks already shipping, so §7.1's unanswered question — whether those two are really era five's and six's — could make this row redundant, and one hour of listening settles it.
- **`era-atomic_age`**: [Cool Vibes](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/Cool%20Vibes.mp3) is this slot's 3-of-3 pick and is deliberately **not** recommended, because its composer's own note is *"Modulates at the midpoint"* and a key change is the learnable event §2 ranks first.
- **`era-information_age`**: this would be the third track by the author of both shipped ones, which for the era whose named risk is characterlessness spends the acquisition without adding a voice.
- **`era-cyberpunk`**: era ten's signature move is a quotation of era one's track, so that half is an edit or a commission whatever is bought here.
- **`crisis-*`**: it sits at the floor of §5.2's 120–240 second band, and whether it is period-neutral enough to play under four eras is the one thing only an ear settles.
- **`crisis-classical`**: second choice [Weight of Responsibility](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/mp3-royaltyfree/Weight%20of%20Responsibility.mp3) (CC-BY 4.0), a brass chorale whose *"nobility and weariness"* is the closest thematic match found to a room of people deciding.

## Verification

**Links: 24 checked, 23 resolved, 1 dead.** The 24 are the 21 cited above, the CC0 1.0 deed, the
Incompetech FAQ the attribution text is read from, and one known-bad URL as a control. **The dead
one is that control and is not cited here**: `mp3-royaltyfree/Cottages.mp3`, the URL that track's
title implies, 404s, because the file is `Magic Scout - Cottages.mp3`. It is checked deliberately,
since a link checker that only ever answers 200 has proved nothing. Separately, **22 media files
were opened with `ffprobe`** and each returned a parsable stream at the duration and byte count the
archived passes recorded, which is a stronger check than a status code.

**Licences were read per asset, not carried forward.** All thirteen OpenGameArt assets cited here —
the eleven picks plus two fallbacks — return `CC0` in the structured `License(s)` field on their own
page, and **none is multi-licensed**, so §6.4's open question touches no row here. The reader was calibrated
both ways first, since one that always answers CC0 proves nothing: it returns `CC0` for the two
shipped tracks and `OGA-BY 3.0 + CC0` for "Feel good island", the field that got that asset
rejected at `CREDITS.md:13-17`. Incompetech's licence is site-wide rather than per asset, and its
FAQ says plainly *"Is this music Copyright Free? No. All of this music is copyrighted"*, so those
three rows are CC-BY 4.0 and **not** public domain. **Not checked: whether any of it sounds right.**
