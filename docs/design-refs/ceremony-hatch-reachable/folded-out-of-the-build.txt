# Is the ceremony's dev hatch in the shipped bundle?

`npm run build -w @cr/web` at the branch tip, then grepping every emitted chunk under
`apps/web/dist/assets/`. A dev control is folded out when its strings are in NO chunk:
`import.meta.env.DEV` is replaced statically, the branch becomes `false && …`, the element is
dropped, the module loses its importer and is tree-shaken out.

The one non-zero row above is the shared eyebrow copy, which the five hatches below ship;
its chunks are SuccessionRoute, HardWinterSurface, PlagueRoute, index, IntrusionRoute and
DiagnosticRunner, and none of them is the ceremony.

## Which dev hatches ship their JS, and which are folded out

    "sx-dev-skip"                          1 chunk(s)
    "hw-dev-skip"                          1 chunk(s)
    "pl-dev-skip"                          1 chunk(s)
    "ob-dev-replay"                        1 chunk(s)
    "dgx-dev-skip"                         1 chunk(s)
    "catchup-dev-skip"                     0 chunk(s)
    "era-dev-skip"                         0 chunk(s)

The first five ship and rely on a runtime check. The catch-up's and the ceremony's are folded
out at build time, and this change keeps the ceremony's in that second group.
