# The city says what it knows: eight frames at 1024x580

The rendered evidence for the three game-feel items #397 landed: a skill building's capacity
line (§4 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-01-game-feel-mechanics-backlog.md`), the first two
crisis milestones (§5), and the copy register behind both.

**Every frame is 1024x580, and the viewport is half the evidence.** That is the window the
team lead reviews on, measured from his own screenshot of a crisis panel bleeding off the
bottom of it. A frame taken at a comfortable developer viewport is exactly as misleading as
arithmetic about `vh` units, and misleading for the same reason: it will look fine.

**Why these are in `docs/` rather than beside their tests.** `.gitignore` ignores
`__screenshots__` at any depth, so every frame these two suites had ever taken was readable
only by the machine that took it. A shot nobody can open is not evidence in a review. Same
precedent as `../crisis-panel-width/` and `../advisor-wardrobe/`.

## What frames 2 and 3 are a pair for, and what the pair cannot show

The pair is the only evidence that the sentence a learner reads actually changes on the beat,
rather than that a function returns a different string. Read together with
`measured-1024x580.txt`: 66,841 pixels differ between them, 11.25% of the window, and looking
at the two pictures says what that is made of — the sentence itself, the `MASTERED` counter
going 0/6 to 1/6, a mastery star filling over the building, and the roster scrolling the
selected row into view. All four are the product working.

**The band the assertion is on holds none of them.** `differs(..., ISLAND_ONLY)` reads a
240x410 rectangle of nothing but island, to the right of the card, the roster and the camera
controls, and it comes back at **0 differing pixels**. That is a claim about whether the
crossing redrew the scene, which the whole-frame number cannot answer at any threshold: tight
enough to be interesting and it fails on the roster scroll, loose enough to pass it and it
stops telling a changed card from a replaced view.

**Both frames are mounted `reducedMotion`, and that is what makes the zero meaningful.**
`StarterIsland`'s gulls are the only whole-frame thing on the render clock, and
`work-log/gabe/2026-08-02-the-photograph-guard-cannot-be-fixed-by-its-floor.md` measured them
at up to **0.88% of the whole frame** between two captures of a scene in which nothing had
changed. Under the prop they hold a posed still and the same comparisons went to exactly zero.

**No frame here shows the completion ANNOUNCEMENT's sentence, and none can.** It goes to a
`visually-hidden` live region, because it is what a screen-reader user is told at a beat a
sighted learner sees as a bounce and a filling star. Captioning frame 3 as though it showed
that sentence would be substituting the cheap evidence for the owed one. What a learner is
told is read out of the DOM by an always-on assertion and recorded verbatim in
`measured-1024x580.txt`:

> Order of operations complete! Its building, Dwellings, is built. Homes for 26 of your people.

## Frame 6 is three frames because one would prove the wrong thing

D-CRISIS-26 lets a winter end unsolved with the damage standing, and §5.4's third guardrail is
that a milestone may never be something a player can miss. Those two together are why the
acknowledgement fires on every outcome and why it is in the report column rather than the
headline slot. A single picture of a clean run says nothing about either. In
`6-conclusion-milestone-lost` the headline is *"Nothing came through the winter."*, all six
buildings read `in ruins`, and the acknowledgement is still there, at body weight, beside the
accuracy line rather than above the verdict.

## Regenerating them

```
VITE_WINTER_SHOTS=1 npx vitest run --config vitest.browser.config.ts \
  apps/web/src/city/cityTellsWhatItKnows.browser.test.tsx

VITE_WINTER_SHOTS=1 npx vitest run --config vitest.browser.config.ts \
  apps/web/src/city/three/winterConclusionSheet.browser.test.tsx
```

**Every frame here is behind `VITE_WINTER_SHOTS`, frame 6 included, and it took a while to get
there.** Frames 1 to 5 were gated for a runtime reason — a shadow-mapped scene with seven GLBs
left on in that suite starves an animation-timing assertion in a different file — and frame 6
was left ungated on the strength of that same reasoning running the other way: *"that suite is
already playing three full winters through the real reducer"*, so the capture costs nothing
extra. True about cost, and cost was the wrong criterion. A plain `npm run check` rewrote these
three tracked PNGs on every run; three agents hit it in one day and restored them by hand, and
the frames were byte-different and picture-identical each time. What a shot flag buys is not
runtime but that a committed file changes only when somebody meant it to.
`test/testWritesStayOutOfTheTree.test.ts` now holds that for every test in the repository.

**One residual, named rather than left to be discovered.** Both suites write these frames and
neither reads them, so nothing in CI notices one being deleted. A guard that read this
directory would be worse than the hole it closed, because `invariants.yml`'s scope filter
classifies `.png` inert and a frame-deleting diff would skip the very step that checked.
