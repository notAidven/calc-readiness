## Coordinator verification of Lane 1 (the new learner copy)

Fixing the P0 meant authoring learner-facing prose — `EXACT_FORM_NOTE`, now shown to 25 asks
that previously refused with `note: null` and so showed only a generic line. That is prose, so

**It holds.** *"Not quite. This one is graded on how the answer is written as well as on its
value, so check yours against the form the answer field asks for."* It claims nothing about
whether the value was right, which is correct and deliberate: there is no comparator behind an

**The `Not quite.` opening is right, not duplicative**, and the reason is worth writing down
because it looks like a defect. `cfu.tsx:433` reads

line rather than appending to it, and a note that omits the verdict leaves the learner without
one. `GENERIC_MISS_NOTE` is mapped back to `null` at `cfuChecker.ts:405` precisely so the card
owns its own fallback, so there is one generic sentence and not two.

Sibling asymmetry noted and left: `NUMERIC_LITERAL_NOTE` and `REDUCED_FRACTION_NOTE` carry no
verdict word, so on those misses the verdict is conveyed only by the card painting the miss.
Consistent enough, and pre-existing.

**A coverage claim I made and then had to withdraw.** I first recorded that this copy had
landed on a surface the prose scanner cannot see, on the strength of the carried-over "3 of 7
documented paths" finding. Measured at this tree, that is **false**: `check:lessons` reports a

## CI, 2026-08-09: one red, and it was ours

`test-unit` **passed at 20m34s**, which settles the Windows triage above from the other side.
`light-gates` and `scope` passed. `code-quality` failed in 3s as the aggregator over a failed
browser job, not on its own account.

`test-browser` failed on exactly **one** of 3045 tests: `cityWithoutWebglShots.browser.test.tsx`,
a file this round added, with *"the district "Geometry" is drawn under `.hud-top-left`"*.

**This is a genuine defect in the round's own HUD label fix, not a flake.** The skyline's start
inset was derived from `--hud-vitality`'s `min(196px, …)`, the widest CHILD of the top-left
cluster, where `.hud-top-left`'s own width is `min(300px, 76vw)`. At 1024x580 the chip column
starts at 224 and the cluster reaches 316, so it shipped 92px inside the capsule.

**The interesting part is why this tree could not see it.** Overlap needs both axes, and the
horizontal miss only becomes a visible collision once the row wraps far enough down to reach the
cluster vertically — which is decided by glyph widths. So a geometric-looking assertion was
actually keyed to text metrics, and it read green on Windows and red on CI's fonts over one
unchanged stylesheet. The rule's own warning, met head-on.

Fix: one token, `--hud-left-width`, read by both the cluster's width and the inset, with both
breakpoint overrides moved onto the token. Guard: the original overlap loop kept, plus a
font-independent clearance assertion, shown red at `expected 224 to be greater than or equal to
316` and green after. Frame re-taken at 1024x580 and looked at — nine district names legible, none
behind the capsule.

## Methodology finding: a first-use census misses later casual uses

Stream E's term census reached five of the six packs that use `range`. It missed `LF.3`
(d7, era 8, six uses) — and that is the **worst** instance in the tree, because it is one
era *after* `FUN.2`'s gloss and tells the learner a model "has a range of inputs", which is
the domain. The census keyed on where a term is first used and first glossed, which is the
right shape for finding an unearned term and structurally blind to a pack that reverts to
the discarded sense downstream of the gloss.

Worth carrying into `.cursor/skills/lesson-qa/SKILL.md`: after a term's meaning is settled,
sweep **every** later use for reversion, not just the introduction sites. Lane 4 found
`LF.3` only because it re-measured the occurrence list instead of taking the census's.
